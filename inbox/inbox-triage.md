# Inbox Triage System

With 120+ unread emails, I would prioritize messages according
to urgency, business impact, and required executive action.

| Priority | Category | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 P1 | Urgent / Executive Action | Handle immediately |
| 🟠 P2 | Important / Time-Sensitive | Handle today |
| 🟡 P3 | Routine / Delegatable | Process or delegate |
| 🟢 P4 | Informational | Review when available |
| ⚫ | Spam / Promotional | Archive/delete |

## P1 — Urgent

Examples:

- Investor communications
- Critical client issues
- Financial deadlines
- Travel disruptions
- Same-day meetings
- Executive escalations

## P2 — Important

Examples:

- Vendor follow-ups
- Client requests
- Scheduling requests
- Internal business updates

## P3 — Routine

Examples:

- Reports
- Administrative requests
- General correspondence

## P4 — Informational

Examples:

- Newsletters
- General updates
- Automated notifications

## Spam

Archive or delete irrelevant promotional messages.

# Key Email Responses

## Email 1 — Urgent Client Request

> Hi [Name],
>
> Thank you for reaching out. I've received your request and
> am prioritizing it for review.
>
> I'll coordinate with the appropriate team and provide you
> with an update as soon as possible.
>
> Best,
> [Your Name]

## Email 2 — Meeting Request

> Hi [Name],
>
> Thank you for reaching out. I'd be happy to coordinate a
> meeting.
>
> Could you please share your preferred dates and times,
> along with your time zone, so I can find the best available
> slot?
>
> Best,
> [Your Name]

## Email 3 — Vendor Follow-Up

> Hi [Vendor Name],
>
> I'm following up regarding [request/order/project] and wanted
> to check on the current status.
>
> Please let me know if there are any updates or if you need
> additional information from our side.
>
> Thank you,
> [Your Name]
