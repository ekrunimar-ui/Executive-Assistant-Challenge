# Travel Contingency Plan

## Situation

The executive's flight scheduled for tomorrow has been canceled,
but the executive must still attend the event.

## Immediate Actions

1. Confirm the flight cancellation.
2. Check alternative flights with the same airline.
3. Check other airlines.
4. Check nearby airports.
5. Compare arrival times.
6. Confirm baggage requirements.
7. Rebook the best available option.
8. Update the executive's calendar.
9. Update the itinerary.
10. Notify relevant parties if arrival time changes.

## Plan A

Book the best available flight that arrives with sufficient
time before the event.

## Plan B

If the preferred flight is unavailable:

- Check another airline.
- Use a nearby airport.
- Arrange ground transportation.

## Plan C

If commercial flights cannot get the executive to the event
on time:

- Contact the event organizer.
- Determine whether remote participation is possible.
- Explore alternative transportation.

## Executive Travel Update

> Travel Alert:
>
> Tomorrow's original flight has been canceled. I am currently
> securing the best alternative that will ensure you arrive
> before the event.
>
> I am checking alternative flights, airlines, and nearby
> airports and will provide the confirmed itinerary once
> finalized.
